The hub did not answer

  1. Is the PC on and awake? Rest mode and sleep both stop it answering.
  2. Is start_hub.bat running? Its window prints the URL to use.
  3. Does that URL match the one above? A PC whose DHCP lease changed will have moved. Give it a static reservation on your router.
  4. Same network? The console and the PC must be on the same LAN — the hub refuses anything outside its subnet allowlist.
  5. Windows Firewall may be blocking the port. Allow Python on private networks.
